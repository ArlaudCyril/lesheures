Explore Hora Essence ↗

One photo at a time

I built the homepage carousel in React and animated the photo transitions with GSAP. React tracks the active photo to keep its caption and the carousel controls in sync.

During the transition, the current photo stays visible beneath the next one. GSAP moves an elliptical mask across the incoming image to reveal it gradually and keep the frame filled. When reduced motion is enabled, the photo changes immediately.
