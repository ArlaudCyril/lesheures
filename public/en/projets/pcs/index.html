Case study · Backend

PCS

A strong customer authentication service that lets PCS prepaid cardholders confirm sensitive operations on a trusted phone.

The problem

For operations that require strong customer authentication under PSD2, PCS needed to verify that the cardholder had confirmed the requested action.

I designed and built the PHP/Symfony backend, from the device registration API to verification of confirmations. I kept the validation rules separate from storage and notification delivery so I could test them independently.

Confirming a payment

Take a payment that needs confirmation. The app shows the amount and merchant. Once the cardholder confirms with biometrics, the registered phone signs a short-lived challenge tied to the payment. The service verifies the signature using the registered public key and returns the result to PCS.

I chose device signing to tie the confirmation to the trusted phone and the payment shown to the cardholder. The private key stays on the phone, and each challenge can be used only once, preventing the same proof from being replayed.

Tests and status

Automated backend tests cover acceptance of a valid signature and rejection of an invalid signature, an expired challenge, or a previously used proof.

The backend was deployed to production on Google Cloud. Checks documented in November 2026 confirmed that the API responded and the background worker was running. End-to-end validation on a phone was still pending at the time of those checks.
