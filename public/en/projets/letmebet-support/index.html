The support agent

The support problem

Questions about challenges and accounts come up repeatedly. To answer them, the team needs to find the right procedure and confirm what happened on each account.

I integrated Claire into the LetMeBet support inbox. I worked on drafting replies from the FAQ, recording which sources the agent received and implementing the rules that flag requests for the team to review.

From ticket to reply

In this fictional example, a customer has completed Phase 2 of their challenge. They can’t see their funded account and ask whether they need to apply.

  1. Intake. The customer opens an in-app ticket. Their message appears in the support inbox.
  2. Context. The system gives Claire the ticket history and the relevant passage from the FAQ section “Funded accounts and withdrawals”. Claire finds that no separate application is needed after passing Phase 2. The system also checks the conversation against the escalation rules.
  3. Draft and handoff. Claire proposes, “You don’t need to submit a separate application once you pass Phase 2. The team will check the status of your funded account and get back to you here.” The system records the source Claire received. In this example, a rule recommends handing the case to the team. The inbox displays that recommendation alongside the draft awaiting approval.
  4. Review and reply. A support team member checks the draft against the records in the admin dashboard. They confirm the challenge is complete and the account is awaiting activation. They add that status to the draft and approve sending the reply in the ticket.

Hermes for internal operations

Summarising progress notes is one example of how Hermes agents can support internal operations. An agent can draft the summary and flag items that need a decision. The team can then check the sources and approve the summary before sharing it.
