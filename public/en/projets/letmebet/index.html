The problem

The product had to combine a consumer challenge experience, multi-sport odds updated in real time and a controlled funding journey.

I worked across interface, server and data: WebSocket, KYC, payments, the joker system and production tooling.

  • Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma and PostgreSQL.
  • Real-time flows for multi-sport odds.
  • A test base covering critical journeys.
